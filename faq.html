FAQs

IPTV UK — Frequently Asked Questions

Devices, activation, speeds, multi-room and trials — the answers UK viewers ask us most.

What devices work — does Firestick Stream work on my TV?

Amazon Firestick & Fire TV, Samsung and LG Smart TVs (use IBO Player on Samsung/LG — see the Samsung & LG setup guide), Android TV/boxes, Android and iOS phones/tablets, Apple TV, Windows, Mac, MAG and Enigma2. If it can run an IPTV player, we can set you up.

How fast is activation?

Usually within 5 minutes. You'll receive login details and setup instructions by email, including the Firestick walkthrough.

Do you offer a free trial?

Yes — a 24-hour free trial so you can test channels, sports and picture quality on your own device and internet before paying. No card required. Claim it on the Free Trial page →

What internet speed do I need?

At least 15 Mbps for HD and 25 Mbps+ for stable 4K. A 5 GHz Wi-Fi connection or an Ethernet adapter for Firestick gives the smoothest live sport. Try the speed checker on the Setup page to see what your connection can handle.

Can I watch on more than one TV at once?

Current plans are 1 connection, so only one screen can stream at a time on a plan. You can install the player and sign in on more than one TV or device, but if a second screen starts playing, the first will stop. For two TVs watching different things at the same time, you would need a separate plan for the second screen.

Is there a contract?

No contract. Plans are prepaid for 1, 3, 6 or 12 months and renew only if you choose. Longer plans simply lower the monthly price.

Xtream Codes vs M3U — which should I use?

Both load the same channels. Xtream Codes (server URL + username + password) is easier — it auto-loads your EPG and VOD. M3U is a single playlist link, handy for apps that don't support Xtream, and the EPG needs a separate link with M3U. We provide both. Read the full Xtream Codes vs M3U guide.

Can I watch live football?

Yes — televised Premier League fixtures are on Sky Sports and TNT Sports, and Formula 1 is on Sky Sports F1, alongside cricket and boxing on the sports channels in the line-up. Check the EPG for the televised schedule on the day.

Do I need a VPN for IPTV?

For most people, no. A VPN only helps if your internet provider is throttling streaming and that throttling is the actual cause of buffering — for example, a fast line where everything else works but IPTV slows at peak times. A VPN also adds overhead and can slow a Firestick down, so fix cache, restart, 5GHz/Wi-Fi or Ethernet and speed first. See the buffering guide.

What happens when my plan ends? Does it auto-renew?

When your plan ends, access simply stops. Plans do not auto-renew and no payment is taken automatically — you choose if and when to renew, using the link in your original email. There is no contract tying you in.

How much does IPTV cost in the UK?

At Firestick Stream, an IPTV subscription UK costs £20 for 1 month, £30 for 3 months (£10/month), £45 for 6 months (£7.50/month) and £65 for 12 months (£5.42/month). Every plan includes the same channels and library on 1 connection, with no contract. Prices elsewhere vary, so compare the total for the period and what is included, not just a headline monthly figure. See Packages.

Is there a free IPTV trial UK and do I need a card?

Yes — Firestick Stream offers a free 24-hour IPTV trial UK with full access to all channels and the movies & series library. No card is needed and there is no contract. Generate a request code on the Free Trial page and quote it on WhatsApp or by email to activate.

What speed do I need for 4K IPTV?

You need 25 Mbps or more for stable 4K, and at least 15 Mbps for HD, measured at the TV. Use 5GHz Wi-Fi or wired Ethernet for live sport in 4K where you can, and try the checker on the Setup page.
